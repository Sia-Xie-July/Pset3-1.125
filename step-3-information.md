| Design decision | Power supply concept | Use the grid as the primary supply, supported by UPS and standby diesel generation. Evaluate dual supply routes and N+1 standby generation, aiming to preserve required capacity after the largest electrical component fails. The detailed configuration remains subject to verification. |
| Design decision | Cooling and water concept | Use direct-to-chip liquid cooling for high-density GPU racks and necessary air cooling for other equipment. | Design decision | Electricity procurement concept | Purchase grid electricity and evaluate renewable electricity contracts. Assume no dedicated onsite solar, wind, or gas generation in the initial operating case. Use standby diesel generation for testing and outages. |
